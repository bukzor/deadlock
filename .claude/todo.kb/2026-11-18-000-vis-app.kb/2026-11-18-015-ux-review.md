# UX review: is the vis UI intelligible, and does it serve the user's goal?

**Parent:** <https:../2026-11-18-000-vis-app.md> (review round 1)

## Problem Statement

The user asked for "a sub-agent to ponder the intelligibility and overall UX
of this UI". They gave the screenshot above. When they first looked at it,
they said, verbatim:

> I have to say I don't understand the result though. What's with the shading?

Think as a Deadlock player who wants to understand an ability's damage over
time and check the numbers in-game. Judge the UI against the user's stated
vision: "a damage versus time graph, with bars that have damage as high, left
side as triggering event, right side as actual time of damage (width as 'wind
up time')", with "various dps measurements (that are checkable in-game)".

Consider at least:
- whether the chart can be read without an explanation;
- the axes and their titles;
- the tooltip;
- the colour;
- how cycles and repeats show;
- the controls: raw keys like `ability_nurse_leech_therapy (cast)`, inputs
  that don't apply to the selected ability, units, defaults;
- the measures table: names like `uptime_dps`, value precision, the length of
  the check text;
- the notes: jargon such as `AbilityCastDelay`, and the upgrade dump;
- empty and error states;
- layout and density;
- dark mode;
- what's missing (a legend or explainer, comparing two abilities, sharing a
  URL with the state).

Skill(dataviz) is disabled for model invocation, as unit 012 found, so apply
dataviz judgement yourself. If you want fresh screenshots, the user's `serve`
is on `http://localhost:8787`.

  `uptime_dps`.
