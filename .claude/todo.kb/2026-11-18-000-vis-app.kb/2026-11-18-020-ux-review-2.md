## Final report

**Player model.** A Deadlock player comes to settle a fight question: how much does this deal in an engagement (2–5 s), how long does it take to kill N HP, and does the card's number hold up? They know the card (Damage, DPS, Duration, Cooldown) and the weapon panel. They don't know "cycle", "period", "horizon" or "trigger". They read the chart first and the top DPS number second. Ranked by harm:

1. **Casts default to a 60 s chart, which crushes the user's own encoding (S; judgement).** *Sees:* Leech's 5 s of damage takes 8% of the width, and the rest is a flat line. A 0.1–0.7 s cast delay is 1–10 px wide at 14 px/s. At the defaults (distance 0, wind-up 0), guns and melee have zero width. *Why:* the user's vision, "width as wind-up", shows in no default view. The fight window the player cares about is a sliver. The cooldown is already one number in the table ("DPS incl. reload/cooldown"). *Fix:* for a cast, default the horizon to the first cast's last land plus about 20%, so 6 s for Leech, and keep `× period` for anyone who wants the cycle. Separately, the user should judge whether a nonzero default gun distance (e.g. 20 m) is worth giving up an exact in-game check.
2. **A hit at exactly the horizon is counted, which inflates every default gun and melee total (S, defect).** *Sees:* Infernus heavy melee shows 3 swings in "the first 2 s", 348 damage, and "DPS over the first 2 s" 174 against a steady 116 (+50%). The gun shows 302.5 against 2×148.5. The extra bar sits on the right axis (`inferno-melee-heavy.png`). *Why:* the default horizon is exactly 2×period, and a source whose first hit lands at t=0 puts the next cycle's first hit at t=horizon. Hits with land ≤ horizon are counted, so it is included. The player checks the number and finds it wrong. *Fix:* count land < horizon (a half-open window), on the server and in the chart's domain alike.
3. **Three DPS numbers, and none is the card's (S).** Round 1's #1 still stands: the card's own values aren't shown. "DPS while active" is the card's DPS diluted by the cast delay (Leech 22.54 vs 23, Flame Dash 28.12 vs 30), and nothing says so. Its label says "≈ stat panel for guns" even on a cast. New: with the 2-period default, "DPS over the first N s" equals "DPS incl. reload/cooldown" for every cast (3.79/3.79, 8.04/8.04), so it is a duplicate, and where it differs, it is finding 2's error. *Fix:* drop the "over the first N s" rows, and show the card's value beside "DPS while active".
4. **Target hp often gets no answer, and when it does, the answer is buried (S).** *Sees:* Infernus gun, hp 600: "not reached within 10.17 s: no time to kill is shown". When the hp is reached (hp 200: 6.03 s), the row comes last in the table, and the chart doesn't mark where the line crosses the dashed hp line. *Why:* typing an hp asks one question, and the page answers it last or not at all. *Fix:* when `hp` is set and `horizon` is not, extend the default horizon to the time-to-kill land (capped at about 120 s). List that row first, and mark the crossing with a `ruleX` and a label.
5. **The chart text is the smallest on the page (S).** *Sees:* the axis titles, the only key that tells which series is "per hit" and which is "cumulative", are Plot's default 10 px. The browser's default 40 px `figure` margins then shrink the SVG further (1216→1136 px at 1280 wide, 909→844 at 988). In the user's shot they are about 5 px. The chart is drawn once at mount, so a resize shrinks it again. *Fix:* `figure { margin: 0 }` and Plot `style: { fontSize: "13px" }`, plus a ResizeObserver redraw.
6. **The inputs and labels use our words, not the player's (S).** *Sees:* `× period`, `horizon`, "Damage per cycle", "DPS incl. reload/cooldown" on melee, and the same check text "(a clip, a swing, a cast)" on every row and every kind. The check column is the widest, most repetitive block on the page. *Fix:* the server labels by kind: "per clip / per cast / per swing", and "DPS incl. reload", "incl. cooldown", or just "DPS" for melee. `× period` becomes "cycles shown", with the placeholder giving the seconds. Each row gets a kind-specific check, or the shared text appears once under the table.
7. **The dual axes line up by accident (S).** Both scales stretch to fill the plot, so bar tops and the line's plateaus coincide by chance. Icebeam's first plateau (225) sits exactly at bar mid-height (2.25), and the gridlines follow the right axis, so the left ticks float between them. *Fix:* give the bars a y domain of 2× the largest hit, so they sit in the lower half as a separate layer, and drop the gridlines or tie them to one axis.
8. **The hero picker is unsorted and has non-heroes (S).** It runs Violet, Holliday, Abrams… and includes `Generic Person` (`in_development`) and `TargetDummy`. *Fix:* sort by name, and filter on `heroes.tsv`'s `in_development` plus the dummy.
