# `apps/vis/client`: the browser view

**Parent:** <https:../2026-11-18-000-vis-app.md> (wave 1)

## Problem Statement

Build the client per the design's "Shape", "Protocol" and "Client view"
sections: a pnpm workspace rooted at `apps/vis/client/`, with Vite, React, TS
strict, Observable Plot and vitest. Load Skill(dataviz) before writing any chart
code.

The server doesn't exist yet. Develop against the protocol as the design
states it:
- `protocol.ts` holds the line types, mirroring the design's tables;
- `api.ts` fetches `/api/{query}` and parses JSONL, streaming line by line;
- tests use fixture JSONL strings.

Vite proxies `/api` to `http://localhost:8787`. Use exact, current package
versions (`pnpm add`), committing `pnpm-lock.yaml`.

Tests:
- JSONL parsing, including a chunk boundary inside a line and a 400 `{"error"}`
  line;
- the mapping from hit lines to Plot marks: `rect` from trigger to land, plus
  `ruleX` at land.

`tsc --noEmit` and vitest must be clean. `node_modules/` and `dist/` are
already gitignored by `apps/vis/.gitignore`.

**Scope (only these files):** everything under `apps/vis/client/`
