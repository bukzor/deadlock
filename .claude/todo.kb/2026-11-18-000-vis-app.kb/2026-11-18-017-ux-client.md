## Decisions

- **Knobs per kind** follow the server's `cycle()` signatures: gun distance,
  melee wind-up, cast spirit; `hp` and the horizon for all. Hidden knobs keep
  their URL value but are not sent.
- **Horizon state:** the URL holds whichever input was typed last (`horizon`
  or `periods`), never both. `periods` becomes seconds through the period
  known for the request minus its horizon; until that is known, the horizon
  is left to the server, and the answer's `cycle` line triggers the real
  request (two fetches only when the base params change with `periods` set).
- **Selection survives an ability change**, typed horizon included: the URL
  shows it, and comparing two abilities at one horizon is a real use.
- **Right axis** shares Plot's one y scale: the cumulative series is scaled
  into the bars' range and the right axis is relabelled (`niceTicks`). No new
  dependency. Gridlines follow the right axis, the main read.
- **Colours:** Tableau 10 blue (bars, left axis) and orange (line, right axis,
  `hp` rule); each axis is drawn in its series' colour.
- **Tooltip:** `Plot.pointerX` over bar midpoints, which picks the bar under
  the pointer for tiled or zero-width bars. Its "wind-up" line is now "width",
  since the width means something else per kind.
- **`hp` scale headroom** (×1.1) keeps the dashed rule and its label off the
  frame's top edge.

## Open Questions

- `pointerX` picks the nearest bar midpoint, so between two adjacent bars of
  very different widths it can name the neighbour. No current source has such
  bars; a containment-based pointer would need a custom render transform.
- The seconds placeholder shows the horizon of the last answer ("default
  10.17"), which is the server's default only while neither input is typed;
  it is hidden whenever a value shows, so it never misleads in practice.
