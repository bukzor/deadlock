# `fixups`: a replacement and a translation for every Japanese Yamato line

**Parent:** <https:../2026-11-24-000-vo-transcripts.md> (wave 5)

## Problem Statement

For every row in `transcripts/asr/yamato.tsv` whose `text` has no row in
`transcripts/fixups.tsv`, write that row: `asr_text replacement translation
doubt by`, with `by` set to your model id. The design doc defines the
columns, and the owner's rulings in the plan define the two texts and
their styling.

Context to use for each line: the event name (`yamato_kill_dynamo_01` says
whom she has just killed; hero internal names map to display names through
the lookup package), and the English captions of the same event family
in `data/vo/lines.tsv`, which often show what the line is about. Yamato is
a samurai-styled hero who took her late brother's name. The register of
her lines is formal and terse.

Set `doubt` when you can't be confident the replacement is what she says,
such as a misheard word that context doesn't settle (「OSIC」). Rows marked
doubtful are escalated: for each one, rerun ASR on that clip alone with
`large-v3-turbo` f16 (the design doc's Escalation bullet):
`./bin/shell python -m bukzor_deadlock.vo_asr yamato turbo CRC32...`. It
renices itself and caps its own threads. Unit 005 found two things you
need:
- **Joined segments:** a clip's segments are joined with no separator, so
  English split mid-sentence loses a space (`select_03-imported`: "this
  namebut I'll"). Fix that in the replacement.
- **Hallucinated asides:** small sometimes adds them, like 「(アルバム)」.
  Drop them.

Until a fix-up exists, `data/vo/japanese.tsv` shows the raw ASR text in
`replacement`; `source` tells the two apart.

One doc item: unit 005 found that brew's whisper-cli is an OpenMP build.
At `-t 4` it runs about 12 threads unless `OMP_NUM_THREADS` matches `-t`
(4.7 s wall and 32.6 s user, against 3.0 s and 10.2 s), so 004's 1×4
throughput row was probably measured while oversubscribed. Add that to
`docs/dev/environment.kb/whisper-asr.md`; the details are in 005's
Decisions. If
the stronger transcript settles it, write a fix-up for it and clear the
doubt. List the clips that stay doubtful in your Final report, for the
owner.

005's run is complete: all 543 rows have landed. When the fix-ups are done,
rebuild `data/vo/japanese.tsv` with redo and commit it with the
ledger.

Spot-check your own output: quote 10 random rows (ASR, replacement,
translation) in your Final report.

**Scope (only these files):** `transcripts/fixups.tsv`,
`transcripts/asr/yamato.tsv` (escalation rows only), `data/vo/japanese.tsv`
(rebuilt by redo), `docs/dev/environment.kb/whisper-asr.md` (the OpenMP
note only), this file.
