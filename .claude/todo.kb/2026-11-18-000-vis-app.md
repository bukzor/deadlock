## Problem Statement

The user asked for this, verbatim:

> Please create an apps/vis subproject, which has client and server components.
> The client runs in the browser with access to the server, the server has full
> access to our data/. You may componentize further if that will help
> maintainability.
>
> The client is ts+React (you may choose vue, nuxt or whatever if you think that
> will be better), a pnpm workspace. The server is python, a uv workspace. Use
> latest python with basedpyright "all" typechecking. See references.kb for
> standards. The communication is jsonl, compatible with jq stdin.
>
> I want this to be general purpose, but just to make it concrete:
>
> **my first goal of many:** Allow selection of character+ability and visualize
> the damage output over time, generate various dps measurements (that are
> checkable in-game). My vision for this is a damage versus time graph, with
> bars that have damage as high, left side as triggering event, right side as
> actual time of damage (width as "wind up time")

## Success criteria

- **Acceptance (the user's):** "I can open a browser and poke at my data". One
  documented command serves the client and the API, reachable from the
  Windows browser.
- `vis-server query timeline hero=… ability=… | jq` works from the shell.
- Every live hero's gun gives a `uptime_dps` and `steady_dps` equal to
  `ability_profiles`' dps and sdps.
- In the browser, picking a hero and an ability draws the bar chart and lists
  the measures and notes.
- `pytest`, basedpyright `all`, `tsc --noEmit` and vitest all pass with no
  errors.
