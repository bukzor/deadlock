# VO transcripts: an English transcript per soundbite

**Method:** Skill(subagent-handoffs); a sub-agent does each unit, briefed in
`2026-11-24-000-vo-transcripts.kb/`

## Problem Statement

Every Yamato VO caption reads "[Speaks Japanese]". Most of the lines are
Japanese and some are heavily accented English. The owner wants an English
transcript for each soundbite, generated by a pipeline.

Where the pieces are (coordinator survey, 2026-11-24):

- Captions: loose KeyValues text files outside the paks,
  `game/citadel/resource/localization/citadel_generated_vo/citadel_generated_vo_<lang>.txt`,
  with readable tokens. The `.dat` that `data/manifest.jsonl` lists is from
  a stale build. `citadel/resource/closecaption_english.txt` is a 340-byte
  stub.
- Event to audio: `soundevents/vo/generated_vo_hero_<hero>.vsndevts_c`.
- Audio: `sounds/vo/...vsnd_c`. The manifest lists 3163 Yamato `vsnd*` paths
  (VO and ability sounds together).
- Host: 12 cores, 7 GiB RAM, no CUDA in WSL. Any ASR runs on the CPU.

The owner's direction: when the data is in the game files but nothing
extracts it yet, build the extraction as we go, starting with one generic
step that explodes files from the VPKs.

The ASR bar ([!@bukzor] 2026-11-24): ASR output must be *intelligible*, not
accurate. A later fix-up pass, with the audio's context, turns it into good
English. So optimize for efficiency:
- Pinning every clip to Japanese is fine, even though some 5 to 10% of the
  "Speaks Japanese" clips are probably English.
- No proper-noun prompting is needed, as long as the output is reliably
  usable.

The fix-up output ([!@bukzor] 2026-11-24): each "Speaks Japanese" clip gets
two texts:
- **replacement:** an accurate rendering of what is spoken, in Japanese,
  styled the way a Japanese reader such as Yamato would expect ([!@bukzor]
  2026-11-24). Agreed reading: Japanese speech in ordinary Japanese
  orthography, with loanwords in katakana (「パワースラッシュ!」). A sentence
  spoken in English goes in Latin script (「Careful, Calico!」).
- **translation:** English that tells English-speaking players what is
  meant.

Extended to every "Speaks <language>" caption ([!@bukzor] 2026-11-24, "yes
let's do those too"): Ivy (`tengu`) has 34 Spanish clips, and Lady Geist
(`ghost`) and Bebop have 1 French clip each. Their replacement is styled
for a native reader of that language.

Caption styling for foreign speech ([!@bukzor] 2026-11-24): the spoken
text as spoken, then the player's-language translation in brackets on the
next line, because "seeing the Japanese as Japanese adds world flavor":

    奇怪な生き物だ。
    [A bizarre creature.]

The same applies to every non-English line. Coordinator's reading,
vetoable: when the spoken language is the player's own (the Japanese
locale for Yamato), the bracket line is dropped. English-spoken lines that
were mislabelled (「Vermin.」) are just the English, with a bracketed
translation in the other locales.

The forum artifact ([!@bukzor] 2026-11-24): a CSV the owner will publish
on the Deadlock forum, one row per clip, with the current caption and the
suggested text and translation. The coordinator recommended the columns
(unit 009's brief).

Pipeline, per line (as settled by units 002 and 003):
1. **The game's English caption,** when it is real. That covers every
   English-spoken clip. No other locale translates the Japanese lines, and
   the sound files embed no text.
2. **ASR, then fix-up,** for the clips captioned "Speaks Japanese":
   whisper.cpp pinned to `-l ja`, on the cheapest setup whose output stays
   intelligible. An agent then writes the replacement and the translation
   from the transcript, the event name and the hero. Where context can't
   settle the replacement (「OSIC」), it marks the row as doubtful, and only
   those clips are rerun on a stronger model, or go to the owner. ML output is not reproducible, so it is
   committed as a ledger keyed by the clip's CRC32 and the model id, and
   only new or changed clips are rerun after a patch.
