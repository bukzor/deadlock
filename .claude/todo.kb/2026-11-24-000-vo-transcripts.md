# VO transcripts: an English transcript per soundbite

**Method:** Skill(subagent-handoffs); a sub-agent does each unit, briefed in
`2026-11-24-000-vo-transcripts.kb/`

## Problem Statement

Every Yamato VO caption reads "[Speaks Japanese]". Most of the lines are
Japanese and some are heavily accented English. The owner wants an English
transcript for each soundbite, generated by a pipeline.

Where the pieces are (coordinator survey, 2026-11-24):

- Captions: `resource/localization/citadel_generated_vo/citadel_generated_vo_<lang>.dat`
  (Valve close-caption files, keyed by CRC32 of the caption token). The
  English and schinese files are in `citadel/pak01`; the other locales have
  their own `game/citadel_<lang>/pak01_dir.vpk`. The loose
  `citadel/resource/closecaption_english.txt` is a 340-byte stub.
- Event to audio: `soundevents/vo/generated_vo_hero_<hero>.vsndevts_c`.
- Audio: `sounds/vo/...vsnd_c`. The manifest lists 3163 Yamato `vsnd*` paths
  (VO and ability sounds together).
- Host: 12 cores, 7 GiB RAM, no CUDA in WSL. Any ASR runs on the CPU.

The owner's direction: when the data is in the game files but nothing
extracts it yet, build the extraction as we go, starting with one generic
step that explodes files from the VPKs.

Pipeline, in order of preference per line:
1. **Text the game ships.** Another locale's caption, or word or phoneme
   data embedded in the `vsnd_c`. This is deterministic and free. Units 002
   and 003 find out whether any of it exists.
2. **ASR.** faster-whisper large-v3 on the CPU, per clip, detecting the
   language (Japanese or English). Japanese lines are transcribed, then
   translated into English by an agent with the hero's context, which beats
   Whisper's own `translate` task. ML output is not reproducible, so it is
   committed as data keyed by the audio's CRC32 and the model id, and only
   new or changed clips are rerun after a patch.
