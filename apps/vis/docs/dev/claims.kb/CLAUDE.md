# claims.kb -- maintenance guide

`apps/vis`'s judgement calls; `../claims.md` is the entry point.

## What belongs here

A choice in `apps/vis` that someone had to make, whose reversal would
change what gets built: the call, the alternative it declined, the owner
grounds it answers to, and what would overturn it. Also the owner's own
rulings, as `user` claims quoting their words.

## What does NOT belong here

The design's content (`docs/dev/decisions.kb/vis-app.md` at the repo
root), facts about game mechanics (`docs/dev/mechanics.kb/`), and how a
unit of work went (the handoff files in `.claude/todo.kb/`).
