# model.kb -- maintenance guide

## What belongs here

A call inside the damage model -- how a source becomes a `Cycle` of
`Hit`s, or how a measure counts them -- made where the data is silent or
ambiguous. Each names the in-game measurement that would overturn it.

## What does NOT belong here

Calls about the stack, the protocol or the client (`..`), and measured
mechanics, which go in `docs/dev/mechanics.kb/` at the repo root.
