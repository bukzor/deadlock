## Decisions

- The witness lives beside the claim as
  `../claims.kb/tooling.kb/event-volume-per-demo.py` (the `{prefix}.py`
  convention of `must-read.kb/before/writing-python-code.md`), not in
  `trash/`, so the `verify:` survives a trash sweep. One file outside the
  brief's literal list; it is the claim's code.
- Without an output dir the witness skips the JSONL/Parquet writes (2.5 GB
  per public demo), so `verify:` costs 70 s and no disk.
- `souls` counted as position rows whose `souls` or `gold_net_worth`
  changed: the bot demo records `souls` as 0 throughout, net worth is the
  fallback.
- `JSON_PYTHON_LIMIT` untouched: positions+damage alone are 1.49M rows on
  the public demo (3x the half-million), 443k on the bot demo; the estimate
  erred low, not an order of magnitude high.
- Ran the demos one at a time: 7 GB of RAM, and one public demo's frames
  approach 2 GB.

## Open Questions

- The witness is `black`-clean but not proven `pyright`-clean: the repo's
  `[tool.pyright]` pins `.venv`, where `polars`/`boon` are absent, so every
  type resolves Unknown (79 findings, all import-rooted). Fix is a
  `uv add boon-deadlock` into some venv pyright sees; out of scope.
- `troopers` (4.1M rows) and `active_modifiers` (213k) dominate "other";
  whether the design ever needs them decides the corpus budget more than
  positions do.

  `tick_to_match_clock` may misbehave there. Not probed.
