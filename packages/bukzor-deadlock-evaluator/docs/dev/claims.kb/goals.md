# goals -- what the evaluator must answer

Every goal here is a comparison between a player's alternatives at a
decision point: a skill point, an item, a whole build. The owner stated
five and asked which nearby goals were missing; the agent proposed
eight. A decision point is a moment a player chooses among options; a
popular build is one many players run, as seen in public build lists
and replays; a decision is dominated when another option is at least as
good in every situation that occurs.
