# Fast and slow layers

Fights are seconds and local; economy and objectives are minutes and
global. Fast outcomes are inputs to the slow layer, and the slow layer
sees only fight summaries. This is the principled reason a Situation
may be a snapshot: it is the fast layer's initial condition, and
transitions between situations are the slow layer's business.
