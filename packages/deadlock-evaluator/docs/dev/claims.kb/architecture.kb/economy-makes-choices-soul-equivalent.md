# economy makes alternatives soul-equivalent

`economy` holds item costs, tiers, slot constraints and resale, plus a
replay-fitted soul-income curve by time and role. It is what turns
"item A or B" into "per soul at this moment" and what constrains the
Builds that search may propose. Pure except for the one fitted curve.
