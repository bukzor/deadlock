# What policy does engage assume?

The policy input to engage (who presses what, when, at whom) is the
largest unsettled design input: simulated outcomes depend on it, and
no data source gives it directly. Candidates are scripted per-hero
rotations, a parameterized policy fit to replays, and a bracket-indexed
family. Settled when calibration against measure shows one is good
enough for the comparisons to come out right.
