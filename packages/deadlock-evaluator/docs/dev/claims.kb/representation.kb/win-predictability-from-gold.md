# Macro state predicts the winner early

In Dota 2 and League of Legends a logistic model on gold difference and
objective difference, conditioned on time, picks the winner roughly 70
to 80 percent of the time by the ten-minute mark. This is the agent's
recollection of the public-analytics literature, uncited, and it is
why V is expected to be smooth and fittable from a modest corpus. A
citation or a measurement on Deadlock replays would make it bare.
