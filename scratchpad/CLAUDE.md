# scratchpad/

Work in progress: notes, prototypes, and captures still being worked on.

Every item graduates, and leaves:
- knowledge to `docs/` (evidence a KB cites into `docs/dev/probes/`);
- code to `src/`;
- the superseded or already-distilled to `trash/` (a recoverable `rm`:
  `mv -t trash/`).

Nothing outside this directory cites it.
